Build a professional, responsive, production-ready garment retail platform for **Rudraksha Collection**, an Indian clothing store. The platform must include a public-facing shopping website, product catalogue, customer accounts, shopping cart, order management, inventory management, billing, sales reports, and a complete CRM admin dashboard. ## 1. Each product must support: - Product ID and SKU. - Product name and description. - Category and subcategory. - Brand. - Product images and gallery. - Purchase cost and selling price. - Discount price and discount percentage. - Available sizes such as XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL. - Colors and product variants. - Fabric and material details. - Stock quantity for each size and color. - Barcode field. - Product tags. - Featured and best-seller status. - Product availability. - Product creation and update timestamps. Include category filters, size filters, color filters, price range filters, sorting, and keyword search. Track stock at the individual size-and-color variant level. ## 4. COMPLETE CRM SYSTEM Build an integrated Customer Relationship Management module accessible through a secure admin dashboard. ### Customer Management Maintain: - Customer ID. - Full name. - Mobile number. - Email address. - Billing and shipping addresses. - Customer registration date. - Date of birth, optional. - Preferred clothing categories and sizes. - Total orders. - Total purchase value. - Last purchase date. - Average order value. - Customer source. - Customer status. - Customer notes and communication history. Automatically create or update customer profiles when an online or in-store sale is recorded. Identify repeat customers and inactive customers based on configurable business rules. Provide customer search by name, mobile number, email, customer ID, or order ID. ### Customer Segmentation Create segments for: - New Customers. - Regular Customers. - VIP Customers. - High-Spending Customers. - Inactive Customers. - Customers with Pending Orders. - Customers Eligible for Offers. - Customers with Previous Returns. Integrate WhatsApp, email, or SMS only through properly configured and authorized providers. Do not claim messages were sent unless the provider confirms success. Obtain appropriate consent for promotional communications. ## 5. SALES AND ORDER MANAGEMENT Implement the complete order lifecycle: Product Selection → Add to Cart → Customer Details → Address → Shipping Selection → Payment → Order Confirmation → Stock Deduction → Packing → Dispatch → Delivery → Feedback. Order statuses: - Pending. - Confirmed. - Processing. - Packed. - Shipped. - Out for Delivery. - Delivered. - Cancelled. - Return Requested. - Returned. - Refunded. Each order must contain: - Unique order ID. - Customer information. - Product details. - Size and color. - Quantity. - Item price. - Discounts. - Applicable taxes. - Shipping fee. - Final payable amount. - Payment status. - Payment method. - Shipping address. - Shipment tracking information. - Order timeline. Generate downloadable invoices and printable order summaries. Prevent duplicate orders from repeated checkout submissions and validate stock before confirming orders. ## 6. PAYMENT AND CHECKOUT Support a configurable payment integration architecture for: - UPI. - Debit and credit cards. - Net banking. - Approved payment gateways. - Cash on Delivery, if enabled. Use a trusted payment provider such as Razorpay or another supported gateway. Verify payment results securely on the server through provider callbacks or webhooks. Never store raw card details, UPI PINs, or payment credentials. Provide payment success, failure, pending, refund, and reconciliation statuses. Allow the administrator to enable or disable payment methods. ## 7. INVENTORY MANAGEMENT Build inventory management with: - Stock by product, size, color, and location. - Opening stock. - Stock received. - Sales deductions. - Returns and restocking. - Damaged goods. - Stock adjustments. - Supplier information. - Purchase orders. - Goods receipt records. - Low-stock alerts. - Out-of-stock alerts. - Inventory valuation. - Stock movement history. Maintain a complete audit trail of stock changes. Prevent overselling through transactional stock validation and appropriate database constraints. ## 8. IN-STORE POS BILLING Create a Point of Sale module for physical garment shop transactions. Features: - Fast product search by name, SKU, or barcode. - Barcode scanning support. - Add items to the billing cart. - Select size and color. - Apply authorized discounts. - Calculate applicable GST using configurable tax rules. - Support cash, UPI, card, and other configured payment methods. - Generate invoice numbers. - Print or download receipts. - Record returns and exchanges. - Update inventory immediately after a completed sale. - Link every sale to a customer when customer details are provided. - Support walk-in customers without forcing registration. Clearly distinguish quotations, pending payments, completed sales, and refunds. ## 9. Do not hardcode fake numbers into production dashboards. Include charts for daily sales, monthly sales, category performance, top products, customer acquisition, and inventory movement. ## 10. REPORTS AND ANALYTICS Generate: - Daily sales report. - Weekly and monthly sales report. - Product-wise sales report. - Category-wise sales report. - Customer purchase history. - Repeat customer report. - Inventory report. - Low-stock report. - Returns and refunds report. - Tax summary. - Payment reconciliation report. - Employee sales report. - Supplier purchase report. - Profitability report. Support CSV and Excel exports where appropriate. Calculate gross profit using recorded cost of goods sold, and clearly distinguish gross profit from net profit. Include refunds, discounts, and relevant expenses according to documented reporting rules. ## 11. EMPLOYEE AND ROLE MANAGEMENT Create role-based access control for: Super Admin: Full system configuration and access. Store Manager: Sales, customers, inventory, reports, and order operations. Sales Staff: POS billing, customer records, and permitted CRM tasks. Inventory Staff: Stock updates, goods receipts, and stock movement. Require authorization for sensitive actions such as refunds, major discounts, stock adjustments, and user permission changes. Record important actions in an audit log. ## 12.
